# Llamadoro Privacy Policy

_Last updated: May 2026_

## Who we are

Llamadoro is a Pomodoro focus timer for iOS and Android, built by Dustin Filippini. Contact: dusty@dustyf.com

## What data we collect — and what we don't

**Llamadoro collects no personal data.**

- No account registration
- No name, email, or phone number
- No location data
- No advertising identifiers or tracking pixels
- No behavioral analytics

All focus session data (session counts, streaks, active llama, settings) is stored **only on your device** using on-device storage. It is never transmitted to any server.

## Third-party SDKs

Llamadoro uses a small number of third-party SDKs, each described below.

### Sentry (crash reporting)

Sentry captures crash reports and unhandled JavaScript errors so we can identify and fix bugs. Sentry may receive:

- Device model, OS version, and app version
- Stack traces from crash events
- A randomly generated anonymous session ID (not linked to any identity)

Sentry receives **no user-identifiable information**. Sessions are anonymous. See [Sentry's Privacy Policy](https://sentry.io/privacy/).

### RevenueCat (in-app purchases)

RevenueCat processes in-app purchase transactions. RevenueCat may receive:

- An anonymous purchase ID generated by Apple or Google
- Your entitlement state (whether you've purchased the full unlock)

RevenueCat does **not** receive your name, email, or Apple/Google account details. See [RevenueCat's Privacy Policy](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy/).

### Apple / Google

In-app purchases are processed by Apple (App Store) and Google (Google Play). Their privacy policies govern that transaction data.

## Data retention

Since all focus data lives only on your device, deleting the app removes all local data permanently. We have no server-side copy to delete.

## Children

Llamadoro is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any information from children.

## Changes to this policy

If we ever add data collection, we'll update this document and note the date. The current version collects nothing.
